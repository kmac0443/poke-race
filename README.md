# Pokémon Race

Train your Pokémon with speedy math, watch it race, and look after it on the farm. Two players, split screen, each
at their own pace. There's no competition between them.

- **Pick a Pokémon:** Eevee, Pikachu, Charmander, Squirtle, Abra, Zorua, Fletchling, Snivy, Ralts, Purrloin, Shinx,
  Riolu or Poliwag. Win races to earn more.
- **Train** in three short games, steering with the movement keys (no typing):
  - ⚡ **Speed**: your Pokémon runs by itself; move into the lane with the right answer, and it goes faster and faster.
  - 💪 **Strength**: pull toward the right answer until the boulder breaks loose.
  - 🛡️ **Defense**: work out where the attack will come from and raise your shield.
- **Race** three new opponents every time. You just watch: the better you trained, the better you do. Win and
  that race is done; there's always a next one.
- **Evolve** toward a goal you can see (and choose which Eeveelution you're going for).
- **The farm:** walk around together, feed and play with everyone's Pokémon by solving little problems, and visit
  your **trophy room**.

**Keys:** player 1 uses W A S D to move, Q for yes and E for no. Player 2 uses numpad 8 4 5 6 to move, 7 for
yes and 9 for no.

You can have one of each Pokémon, but Eevee is special: you can raise one Eevee for each Eeveelution, chosen when
you pick it.

For parents: `admin.html` sets each child's math range, their own Pokémon list, evolution goals and pacing, and
fixes progress.

Status: **early.** Both players can pick who they are and a first Pokémon, then walk around the farm together
with their Pokémon following them. Training, races and the trophy room come next. The plan is in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## Run it

Double-click `start.bat`, or run `node server.js` and open http://localhost:8081 (admin: http://localhost:8081/admin.html).

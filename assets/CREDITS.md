Everything in `assets/` came from the sister project `../pokemon-adventure` (see its `dist/assests/CREDITS.md`),
which took it from:

## Pokémon Essentials v21 (via Manurocker95/Pokemon-Essentials-21-With-Unofficial-EBDX)
https://github.com/Manurocker95/Pokemon-Essentials-21-With-Unofficial-EBDX
Licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (Pokémon Essentials). Credit: Maruno, Poccil, Flameguru and the Essentials contributors.

Sprites (run sheets, front sprites, icons, eggs), tilesets, autotiles, battle backgrounds, animation sheets,
weather/fogs, object and people sheets, berry trees, item icons, ribbons, PWT pictures, windowskins, fonts, cries,
sound effects, jingles, and music. `data/species.json` and `data/tilesets.json` were converted from the Essentials
PBS files and `Tilesets.rxdata` (same licence).

## Other
- **Front sprites after Gen 5**: Sylveon (`pokemon/front/700.png`, via the adventure game) and Fletchling,
  Fletchinder, Talonflame (`661–663.png`, kept in `tools/extra-sprites/front/`). Gen-5-style fan sprites from the
  [Smogon sprite project](https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/sprite-project.3647722/), as served by
  [Pokémon Showdown](https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/gen5/).
- **Music**: the `.ogg` tracks were rendered from the Essentials MIDI files with [FluidSynth](https://www.fluidsynth.org/)
  and the [GeneralUser GS](https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS) soundfont (S. Christian Collins).
  `Cycling Road.wav` is an original tune from the adventure game's `tools/chiptune.py`.
- **Lava autotile** (`autotiles/Lava.png`): made for the adventure game by recolouring `Sea without shore.png`.
